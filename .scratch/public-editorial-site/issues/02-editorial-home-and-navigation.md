## Reviewed wording requirements

Use the complete [Home draft](../copy-review.md#proposed-home-copy), [shared wording](../copy-review.md#shared-wording-and-adjacent-application-text) and [metadata](../copy-review.md#proposed-metadata), informed by [product marketing context](../../../docs/product-marketing.md). Alternative headlines and CTAs are not implementation instructions.

- [ ] Opening heading: “Some things matter more. Which things matter to you?” Supporting copy: “Sort value cards to explore your priorities. Narrow your choices, then reflect on what your final set means to you.” Actions: “Find my core values” and “See how it works”. Returning-user link: “Resume an exercise in History”.
- [ ] Use Learning, Independence and Helping others for the labelled Example outcome, with catalogue descriptions unchanged. Label the note “Illustrative note for Learning” and use “I want room to learn in whatever I do next.” State that the selection is an example and the visitor chooses their final count.
- [ ] Use the reviewed situations section, “When a decision pulls you in different directions”, and the complete Sort/Narrow/Reflect descriptions. Describe possible uses, not attributed customer experiences or guaranteed outcomes.
- [ ] Use the reviewed secondary behaviour invitation and closing section. Replace “What do others see that I don’t?” with “See your choices from another angle”; explicitly describe possible perspectives rather than knowledge of other people's thoughts.
- [ ] Use warm, confident, natural British English. Explain reader benefits through the actual card sort and revisitable choices; avoid corporate phrasing, hype, clichés, formulaic marketing language and invented claims.
- [ ] Implement the reviewed Home title and description, with matching social descriptions. Keep canonical URLs and existing share destinations.
