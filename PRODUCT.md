# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individuals first, using the app to identify and prioritise their personal values. The app also supports team-building use.

## Product Purpose

Help people identify and prioritise their core values through an interactive card-sorting exercise and guided reflection.

## Positioning

The product's method is a values card sort followed by reflection on the selected values. No broader market distinction is established.

## Operating Context

The app supports self-guided personal development and can also be used for team-building.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Users can define, sort, and prioritise values, then reflect on their selections.
- Session data is stored in the browser.
- The app does not currently provide collaborative team features.

## Brand Commitments

- For the favicon and background assets, derive motifs from `public/images/core-values.png`, especially its heart, sprout, leaves, and branching forms.
- Recolor those motifs with the existing calm plum, rose, and warm neutral palette in `src/styles/palette.css`; do not carry over the source image's bright reds, oranges, greens, or teal.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product description and known limitations in `README.md`.
- Source artwork in `public/images/core-values.png`.
- Existing palette in `src/styles/palette.css` and background artwork in `public/images/`.

## Product Principles

- Give people a clear way to identify and prioritise their own values.
- Support reflection after sorting so users can connect their selections to decisions.
- Preserve personal choice; the app guides the exercise rather than assigning values.
